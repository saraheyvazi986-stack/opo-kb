Contact and Office Information

Simple explanation

This note documents the official channels for contacting OPO, the physical office locations of the various group entities, and the support availability that clients can rely on. Use the right channel for the right purpose — different inquiries have different recommended routes, and using the appropriate channel typically gets a faster, more accurate response.

Email addresses

OPO maintains several specialized email addresses for different inquiry types:

General customer support

support@opo.com

Use for:

  • Account access issues (login problems, password resets)
  • Platform questions (how to use MT4/MT5/cTrader features)
  • Trading questions (how to place orders, modify positions)
  • General product information
  • Technical issues with the trading platform
  • Withdrawal status inquiries
  • Deposit confirmation issues
  • Refund requests

This is the default channel for most trader inquiries. The OPO website specifically notes: “For refunds please email to (support@opo.com) and withdrawals take up to 1 business day.”

KYC and verification

KYC@opo.com

Use for:

  • Submitting KYC documents (when not done through the dashboard)
  • Questions about document requirements
  • Document expiry and renewal questions
  • Address change documentation
  • Questions about Close-Only Mode
  • KYC-related rejections or clarifications

This is the dedicated channel for verification matters. See KYC and Account Activation for the full process.

Partnerships and business development

partners@opo.com

Use for:

  • Introducing Broker (IB) program inquiries
  • Affiliate program applications
  • Business partnership proposals
  • Volume-based trading relationships
  • Institutional client inquiries
  • Money manager arrangements

This is the channel for any commercial relationship beyond standard retail trading.

Other specialized channels

OPO may operate additional specialized email addresses (legal, compliance, regulatory) that are typically used internally or in formal proceedings. For routine matters, the three addresses above cover virtually all client needs.

For voice support outside these hours, traders should use email or live chat. Live chat is described as available 24/7 according to the website, though staffed availability outside business hours may be limited.

Live chat

Live chat is accessible directly from the OPO website (OPO.com) and from the client dashboard. The chat system is operated through LiveChat, an established customer service platform.

Live chat is described as 24/7 on the website. Practical experience suggests:

  • During business hours (GMT+3, 8 AM - 5 PM): typically staffed by human agents with quick response times
  • Outside business hours: may be staffed by overnight agents, automated bots, or queue for next business day response
  • Response quality is generally good for routine inquiries

Live chat creates a written record that the client can save (typically through the chat interface’s “email transcript” feature). This is useful for documenting important communications.

Web form and direct contact

The OPO contact page (opo.com/company/contact-us) includes a web form for inquiries. Per the website: “Fill this form, we will check your request in less than 24 hours.”

This is functionally similar to email but routes inquiries through OPO’s internal ticketing system. For most matters, direct email is more straightforward.

Which channel for which situation

A practical decision guide:

Use live chat when

  • You need an immediate answer to a simple question
  • You’re stuck during account setup or platform usage
  • You want a quick confirmation of something
  • You can sit at a computer or phone while waiting for response

Use email (support@) when

  • You need a documented record of communication
  • The matter is non-urgent
  • You’re attaching screenshots or documents
  • The matter is complex enough to require considered response
  • You’re operating outside business hours

Use email (KYC@) when

  • Anything specifically about identity verification, address proof, or document status
  • Document expiry questions
  • Re-verification matters

Use email (partners@) when

  • Discussing any commercial relationship beyond standard retail trading

Use phone when

  • You prefer voice communication for a specific reason
  • The matter involves dialogue rather than information exchange
  • You’re in a time zone with overlap to GMT+3 business hours

Use the formal complaints procedure when

  • You have a specific grievance about OPO’s actions
  • Previous support interactions haven’t resolved an issue
  • You need a formal written response with regulatory weight
  • See Complaints Procedure for full process

Use social media for

  • Following OPO announcements and news
  • Community discussions with other traders
  • Marketing-related questions (offers, promotions, events)
  • Not for account-specific or sensitive matters

What to include in any communication

For faster, more accurate responses, traders should include:

  1. Full name matching the account
  2. Client ID or account number (visible in the dashboard)
  3. Specific account if you have multiple (MT4, MT5, cTrader, etc., with account numbers)
  4. Clear description of the question or issue
  5. Specific times, dates, and transaction numbers when relevant
  6. Screenshots of error messages or platform displays when relevant
  7. What you’ve already tried if you’ve attempted self-help

Including this information upfront prevents back-and-forth and gets to resolution faster.

What to keep records of

For any significant communication with OPO, traders should keep their own copies:

  • Save email threads
  • Save live chat transcripts (use the email-transcript feature)
  • Record phone call dates, times, and the substance of conversations
  • Screenshot any in-platform notifications
  • Keep ticket numbers or reference numbers when provided

This is essential if a matter later requires escalation through the Complaints Procedure or external dispute resolution.

Security considerations for communication

A few important security points:

OPO support will never ask for:

  • Your password
  • Your full credit card number
  • Your security questions and answers in unmasked form
  • Authentication codes (2FA codes)

Any communication claiming to be from OPO and asking for these is fraudulent. Legitimate support can resolve account issues without needing your password.

Verify communications:

  • Official OPO emails come from @opo.com addresses
  • Be skeptical of emails from similar-looking domains (opo.com, opo.com, etc.) — these are common phishing patterns
  • The official website is OPO.com — verify the URL carefully

Protect your account:

  • Use a unique, strong password for OPO
  • Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) if available
  • Never share login credentials with anyone, including someone claiming to be OPO support
  • Be cautious of “managed account” or “trading assistant” offers from individuals claiming to work with OPO — verify any such relationships directly with official OPO channels

Risk warning

Communication channel choice matters more than traders typically realize. Account-sensitive matters discussed through unofficial channels (random WhatsApp contacts, Telegram groups, third-party “support” services) create real risks — these are common vectors for fraud where scammers impersonate broker support staff. For any matter touching your account, funds, or KYC documents, use only the official channels documented in this note: official email addresses ending in @opo.com, the phone number on the official website, the live chat on OPO.com, or the contact form on OPO.com. If anyone — including someone claiming to be OPO staff — contacts you through other channels asking for account credentials, funds, or personal information, treat the contact as suspicious and verify through official channels before responding. Genuine OPO support can be reached at the channels listed above; impersonators cannot.
