# Social Trade Account

## Simple explanation

The Social Trade Account is OpoFinance's copy-trading offering. It comes in two tiers: a Standard tier for traders new to copy trading (\$200 minimum, no commission) and a Social Pro tier for higher-volume copy traders (\$5,000 minimum, lower commission and tighter spreads). Both run on MT4, MT5, and the Web Terminal, and connect to OpoFinance's Social Trade Platform where traders can follow or copy strategies from experienced traders in the community.

## Who this account is for

The Social Trade Account suits traders who want to copy or follow other traders rather than (or in addition to) trading independently. The Standard tier is the entry point for traders who are still learning the market and want to follow experienced traders for guidance. The Social Pro tier is for traders who copy at higher volumes or want the same trading economics as ECN Pro while keeping access to the Social Trade Platform.

Traders who want to *be copied* (sharing their strategies with the community) can also use these accounts to showcase performance.

## Copy trading risk warning

Copying or following other traders does not eliminate the risk of loss. Past performance of a copied trader is not indicative of future results. The trader being copied may change their strategy, take on more risk, or stop trading at any time. Copied trades may execute at different prices than the original trade due to slippage or latency. Traders should evaluate the risk profile of any trader they choose to copy and never invest more than they can afford to lose.

## General risk warning

Trading CFDs with leverage involves a high level of risk and may result in losses greater than the initial investment. Traders should fully understand how leveraged products work and consider seeking independent advice before opening an account.
