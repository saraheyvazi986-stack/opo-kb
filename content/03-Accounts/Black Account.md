# Black Account

## Simple explanation

The Black Account is OpoFinance's VIP-tier account, designed for ultra-high-net-worth individuals and institutional traders. It requires a $100,000 minimum deposit and offers raw spreads with zero commission — meaning OpoFinance earns through the spread only. Available on MT4 and MT5.

## Who this account is for

The Black Account is designed for ultra-high-net-worth individuals, institutional traders, and family offices placing significant volume. The $100,000 minimum deposit makes it inaccessible for retail traders, and the value proposition — raw spreads with no commission — is most beneficial for traders who place large lot sizes where the spread cost is the dominant factor.

Traders who place smaller volume but with similar capital should consider whether [[ECN Pro Account]] (with $4 commission and 0.0 pip spreads) is more economical, since paying a per-lot commission may cost less than the implicit cost embedded in the raw spread at typical retail trade sizes.

## How Black differs from ECN Pro

Both accounts target high-deposit traders, but with different cost models:

| | ECN Pro | Black |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum deposit | $5,000 | $100,000 |
| Commission | $4 per lot | No commission |
| Spread | From 0.0 pip | From 0.0 pip (raw) |
| Best for | High-volume professional | Ultra-high-net-worth, institutional |

The economics depend on lot size and trading frequency. A trader paying $4 commission on ECN Pro versus paying an implicit spread cost on Black should compare actual cost-per-trade based on their specific trading pattern before choosing.

## Risk warning

Trading CFDs with leverage involves a high level of risk and may result in losses greater than the initial investment. The Black Account's high minimum deposit allows for substantially larger position sizes than other accounts, which amplifies both gains and losses. Traders using high leverage on a $100,000+ account can incur losses that exceed their deposit very quickly during volatile market conditions. Traders should fully understand how leveraged products work, consider their risk tolerance carefully, and consider seeking independent financial advice before opening a Black Account.
