# Standard Account

## Simple explanation

The Standard Account is OpoFinance's entry-level trading account, designed for new and experienced traders who want a straightforward CFD trading experience without commissions. It's available on MT4, MT5, the Web Terminal, and OpoTrade, with a low $100 minimum deposit.

## Who this account is for

The Standard Account is suitable for traders who prefer commission-free trading and accept slightly wider spreads in return. It works well for newer traders learning the market, and for swing or long-term traders whose trading frequency is low enough that the spread cost is acceptable.

## Risk warning

Trading CFDs and leveraged products involves a high level of risk and may result in losses greater than the initial investment. Traders should ensure they fully understand how leveraged products work and consider seeking independent advice before opening an account.
